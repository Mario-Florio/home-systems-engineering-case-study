# Home Systems Engineering Case Study

*A longitudinal engineering case study documenting the application of systems engineering methodology to the development of a residential home system.*

---

## Overview

This repository documents the development of a residential home using an adapted systems engineering methodology inspired by Kossiakoff and Sweet's *Systems Engineering: Principles and Practice*.

The project serves two complementary purposes:

* **A case study** demonstrating the application of systems engineering to the residential domain.
* **A reusable methodology** that homeowners, engineers, and researchers may adapt to similar projects.

Ultimately, this work seeks to explore whether rigorous systems engineering practices can improve decision making in domestic household planning, construction, and operation.

---

## What is Home Systems Engineering?

Home Systems Engineering is the application of systems engineering principles to the design, improvement, operation, and maintenance of domestic households.

Rather than viewing a home as a collection of independent projects, Home Systems Engineering treats the home as an integrated supersystem whose subsystems interact through shared resources, constraints, and stakeholder objectives.

Examples include:

* Food production
* Water collection
* Energy generation
* Waste management
* Climate control
* Financial planning
* Storage
* Maintenance planning

By considering these systems collectively, decisions can be evaluated according to measurable performance, lifecycle cost, stakeholder needs, and long-term sustainability rather than intuition alone.

---

## Methodology

The development process follows an adaptation of the systems engineering life cycle described by Kossiakoff and Sweet.

Development progresses through three major stages:

```
Concept Development
        ↓
Engineering Development
        ↓
Post-Development
```

Each stage is further decomposed into progressively refined development activities.

Throughout every stage, development follows the Systems Engineering Method:

1. Requirements Analysis
2. Functional Definition
3. Physical Definition
4. Design Validation

This iterative process maintains traceability between stakeholder needs, functional behavior, physical implementation, and verification throughout the project.

### Documentation

The `docs` directory contains the engineering documentation produced throughout the study.

Documents are organized according to their position within the development life cycle.

Each document generally corresponds to an atomic engineering activity and is version controlled through Git, allowing the repository history to document the evolution of the system rather than only its final state.

---

### Templates

The `templates` directory contains reusable document templates adapted for Home Systems Engineering.

These templates are intended to allow others to reproduce or adapt the methodology for their own domestic household engineering projects.
