# Operational Analysis

**Purpose:** Understand operational objectives of system

**Input:**

* Operational deficiencies
* Technological improvements
* Predecessor system

**Outputs:** Operational objectives

---

## Stakeholder Needs

**Purpose:** Provide context for deficiency analysis and generate system insights

### Production Capacity

* Overall land capacity (e.g., food, water, electricity, etc.) is productive enough to sustain part-time employment, or ideally less

> **Note:** Inhabitant demand assessment is separate from stakeholder needs. See *Deficiency Analysis* for overview of household resource needs.

### System Environment

* Location has reliable communication services to outside world
* Property is relatively private / secluded
* Location has job market demand for *select employment* (information detracted for client anonymity)
* Location has reliable internet connection with adequate throughput

### Investment

* *Hundreds of thousands* upfront investment capability (> ~$300,000)
* *Tens of thousands* recurring annual cost (> ~$70,000)

### Labor

* No more than *several* hours of *moderate to strenuous* labor daily
* Preference for *household labor* to *employment labor*

---

## System Insights

### Production

* High *feasibility* (;.e., self-sufficiency) is good
* Low *capital dependence* is favorable
* Excessive overall capital cost is detrimental to success

### Lifestyle

* Low local population density is preferable
* Local economy and job market is significant to success
* Moderate labor willingness

---

## Operational Requirements

* Land capacity must produce enough to support inhabitant demand
* Upfront cost must remain in budget (*hundreds of thousands*)
* Recurring capital costs must not require more than part-time income
* Local population must be low density
* Local and regional job market must provide select oppurtunity

---

## Operational Objectives

* Land produces resources (food, water, electricity, etc.) greater than or equal to inhabitant demand
* Initial investment is less than ~$300,000
* Overhead capital cost is less than or equal to local part-time income
* Overhead labor cost is less than or equal to ~8 hours daily
* System environment (e.g., land area, topography, foiliage, population density, etc.) enables private living conditions
* System environment (e.g., economy, job market, etc.) supports select employment

---

## Formal Model

*`Land Capacity - Inhabitant Demand ≤ Part Time Income`*
