### Needs Analysis

**Steps:**

1. Operational Analysis
2. Functional Analysis
3. Feasibility Definition
4. Needs Validation

---

#### Operational Analysis

***Artifacts:***

* Deficiency Studies (Current State Analysis)
* Operational Objectives

> *Stakeholder Need Elicitation* may be used to aid in deriving Operational Objectives.

---

#### Functional Analysis

***Artifacts:***

* Subsystem Functional Interface Definitions
* System Envirionment Interface Definitions

---

#### Feasibility Definition

***Artifacts:***

* Feasibility Studies (Feasibility Criteria, Candidate Concept, Compatibility Assessment)
* Feasible Concept (Subsystem Techonology Design, Concept of Operations)

---

#### Needs Validation

***Artifacts:***

* Operational Effectiveness Analysis (Performance Parameters, Measures of Effectiveness)
* System Operational Requirements
